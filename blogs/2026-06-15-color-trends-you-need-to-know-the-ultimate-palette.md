From grounding browns and warm cinnamons to expressive plums and optimistic blues, today’s most relevant hues signal a shift toward authenticity, emotional depth, and refined self-expression. Through AI-powered trend forecasting, brands can move beyond intuition, identifying both major color movements and nuanced tonal evolutions early, and transforming data-driven color intelligence into a strategic advantage for long-term product and collection planning.
