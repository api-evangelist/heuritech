Capsule collections offer fashion brands flexibility, allowing them to explore new trends or collaborations between main seasonal drops. Successful launches depend on aligning three factors: rising consumer demand, manageable production timelines, and sufficient retail space. A strategic approach focuses on forecasting future demand rather than responding retroactively to trends, enabling effective testing and experimentation.
