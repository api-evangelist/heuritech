Capri pants are making a powerful comeback in 2026, evolving far beyond their early 2000s identity. From sleek capri leggings to voluminous cropped silhouettes, the trend reflects fashion’s growing focus on movement, proportion, and expressive styling. Backed by strong growth data across Europe and the US, capris are re-emerging as a key summer staple, blending nostalgia with a modern, fashion-forward edge.
