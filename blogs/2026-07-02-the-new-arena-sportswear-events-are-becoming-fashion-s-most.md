Major sporting events, like Grand Slam tournaments and the FIFA World Cup, have become significant platforms for brand activations and trendsetting in fashion. Fashion influences draw on various inspirations beyond traditional runway shows, including athletes’ style, fan attire, and cultural engagement. Brands aim to create emotional connections with audiences through sports, leveraging global visibility and cultural significance to enhance consumer adoption and engagement.
