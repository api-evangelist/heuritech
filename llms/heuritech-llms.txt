# Heuritech

> Heuritech is a Paris-based AI company (Heuritech SAS, founded 2013, part of the Luxurynsight group
> since December 2024) that applies computer vision and deep-learning demand forecasting to roughly one
> million social-media posts a day from Instagram, Weibo and TikTok, recognising more than 2,000 fashion
> attributes to quantify and predict consumer demand for apparel, footwear and luxury brands up to a
> year ahead.

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-08-17. Heuritech does not publish an llms.txt of its own
(https://heuritech.com/llms.txt returns 404), so this file was generated from the profile in
apis.yml and the artifacts in this repository.

## What is and is not callable

- The **Heuritech Trend Data API** is a real commercial product, sold on the Business (from 35k€/year)
  and Enterprise plans. It is **entirely sales-gated**: no developer portal, no API reference, no base
  URL, no authentication documentation, no specification and no SDK are published anywhere. Access
  begins with a demo request. Do not attempt to construct calls to it.
- The **only machine-readable surface a member of the public can reach** on heuritech.com is the
  WordPress REST API that serves the company's content estate, plus an OAuth-gated MCP server.

## APIs

- [Heuritech Trend Data API](https://heuritech.com/heuritech-market-insights/): weekly data points per
  product attribute (prints, colours, fabrics, shapes), up to 6 years of history and 2 years of
  forecast, across 1,000+ trends. Sales-gated; no public contract.
- [Heuritech MCP Server](https://heuritech.com/wp-json/mcp/mcp-oauth-server): live remote MCP endpoint
  over the content estate. OAuth 2.1 + PKCE, single `mcp` scope. Anonymous `tools/list` returns 401
  `mcp_unauthorized`; the tool inventory is not publicly observable.
- [Heuritech Content API (WordPress wp/v2)](https://heuritech.com/wp-json/wp/v2): anonymously readable
  JSON over 169 blog posts, 63 pages, 3,800 media items, 1,284 comments, 5 categories, 25 authors and
  a 232-item site search.
